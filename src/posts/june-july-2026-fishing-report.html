Summer has arrived in Key West, and the fishing is as good as it gets.

June and July bring outstanding opportunities across every fishery—from the calm waters of the Backcountry to the reefs, wrecks, and blue water offshore. Whether you're chasing a bucket-list Tarpon, filling the cooler with Snapper, or heading offshore for Dolphin and Sailfish, this is one of the most productive times of the year to be on the water.

Offshore Fishing

Offshore action continues to improve as summer settles in.

While the peak Sailfish spawn has wrapped up, we continue finding fish just beyond the reef. These powerful predators stay on the move, making them an exciting challenge for anglers willing to put in the time. Adding Dolphin (Mahi) to the day's plan often creates outstanding opportunities for multiple offshore species.

Blackfin Tuna remain consistent, providing quality fish and exciting action, while Dolphin fishing becomes the highlight of the summer. Large schools of Mahi are moving through offshore waters, offering fast-paced action, incredible colors, and excellent table fare.

Large Barracuda are also active throughout the summer, adding another hard-fighting predator to the mix.

If offshore fishing is your priority, we generally recommend booking at least a 3/4-day (6-hour) trip. The additional fishing time allows us to reach productive water and maximize opportunities throughout the day.

Reef & Wreck Fishing

Summer weather typically brings calmer seas, making reef and wreck fishing an excellent option for anglers looking for comfortable conditions and steady action.

Grouper season continues strong with opportunities for Red, Gag, and Black Grouper. Cobia remain a possibility throughout the summer, while Amberjack continue producing on many of the deeper wrecks south of Key West.

The Snapper bite is one of the biggest highlights this time of year. Mangrove, Mutton, and Yellowtail Snapper are providing excellent action, with the summer Mangrove Snapper spawn producing some truly impressive fish. Around the July full moon and into August, large schools gather on the reefs and feed aggressively.

Permit fishing also remains a favorite for anglers looking to test their skills on one of the Keys' most respected game fish.

For those looking for a little extra excitement, Barracuda and Sharks continue to provide outstanding light-tackle battles throughout the reef system.

Backcountry Fishing

The Backcountry continues to shine during June and July.

Protected waters, scenic flats, and steady action make it a great choice for families, first-time anglers, and experienced fishermen alike. With calm conditions and plenty of variety, it's one of the most enjoyable ways to spend a day on the water.

Tarpon remain the headline attraction.

The annual migration is well underway, and schools of Silver Kings continue moving through the shallows. Early mornings and evenings remain the most productive feeding windows, with moving water playing a major role in successful trips.

When tides line up, our Sunset Tarpon trips can produce unforgettable action as these fish move shallow to feed before dark.

For anglers focused solely on Tarpon, our Split Tarpon charters allow us to fish both prime feeding windows—early morning and late afternoon—giving anglers the best opportunity to connect with one of Key West's most iconic game fish.

Beyond Tarpon, the Backcountry continues producing Cobia, Mangrove Snapper, Yellowtail Snapper, Barracuda, and several species of Sharks. Predator fishing is especially strong during the summer months, particularly around the Marquesas, where large Sharks and giant Barracuda provide unforgettable battles on light tackle.

Marquesas Atoll

Summer is also an outstanding time to make the run west to the legendary Marquesas Atoll.

Crystal-clear water, remote flats, abundant Tarpon, aggressive Sharks, and giant Barracuda make this one of the most unique fisheries anywhere in the Florida Keys. For anglers looking to experience something beyond the typical charter, the Marquesas continue to deliver unforgettable days on the water.

Summer Fishing Outlook

Warmer, more stable water temperatures have created consistent fishing conditions across every fishery.

Morning trips generally offer the best opportunities in the shallows, while afternoon reef trips continue producing quality action in slightly deeper water. For guests looking to stay in calm conditions while enjoying plenty of action, summer afternoons remain an excellent choice.

No matter where you fish, June and July consistently rank among the best months of the year in Key West.
